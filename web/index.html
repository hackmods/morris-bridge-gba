How to play

  • Title: Start → pick a track
  • Tracks: 8 Sodor gaps — weld a deck, then Start sends the train
  • Build: Hold A and drag to weld a span; R swaps track/rod where allowed

  • Undo: L removes your last edit
  • Sir Topham Hatt judges your work — avoid confusion and delay
